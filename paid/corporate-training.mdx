Every organization — regardless of size — depends on two layers of security training: broad awareness programs that reduce human risk across the entire workforce, and deep technical training that sharpens the skills of your security team. A single employee clicking a phishing link or a misconfigured cloud resource can undo years of technical investment, which is why organizations are increasingly treating training not as a compliance checkbox but as a core security control. The platforms below address both layers, from company-wide phishing simulations to elite red team skill development.

## Security Awareness Training

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="KnowBe4" icon="envelope" href="https://knowbe4.com">
    The industry leader in security awareness training and simulated phishing. KnowBe4 allows you to run realistic phishing campaigns against your own employees, track click rates over time, and assign targeted training to users who fall for simulations. Used by tens of thousands of organizations worldwide.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Proofpoint Security Awareness" icon="shield-halved" href="https://proofpoint.com">
    An enterprise-grade awareness platform that combines threat intelligence data with training content, allowing you to tailor simulations based on real attack trends targeting your industry. Strong analytics and executive reporting make it a favorite for large organizations.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Cofense" icon="fish" href="https://cofense.com">
    Specializes in phishing defense training and employee-reported phishing workflows. Cofense's PhishMe platform conditions employees to recognize and report phishing emails, and integrates with security operations teams to act on those reports in real time.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Terranova Security" icon="globe" href="https://terranovasecurity.com">
    Offers a comprehensive content library of multilingual security awareness modules, phishing simulations, and compliance training. Terranova's programs are particularly well-suited for global organizations that need consistent training across diverse regions and regulatory environments.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Technical Team Training

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Cybrary for Teams" icon="users" href="https://cybrary.it/teams/">
    Provides role-based learning paths, customizable training plans, and detailed reporting dashboards designed specifically for security team leads and managers. Map your team's training to specific job roles — SOC analyst, incident responder, cloud security engineer — and track progress at scale.
  </Card>

  <Card title="SANS OnDemand" icon="building" href="https://sans.org/ondemand/">
    Delivers GIAC-aligned SANS courses on a flexible, self-paced schedule — ideal for security professionals who can't attend live events. Organizations can purchase seat licenses for multiple team members and track completion across high-value courses like SEC504 and SEC560.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Offensive Security Enterprise" icon="crosshairs" href="https://offensive-security.com">
    Brings OffSec's world-class offensive security training to enterprise teams with volume licensing for PEN-200 and other courses. Ideal for organizations building or scaling an internal red team, with custom lab environments and dedicated support.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Immersive Labs" icon="flask" href="https://immersivelabs.com">
    Goes beyond individual skill training to provide team-based crisis simulations and cyber resilience exercises. Immersive Labs measures team-wide capability across technical, leadership, and communications dimensions — helping organizations identify systemic gaps before an incident exposes them.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Before signing an enterprise contract, check whether your organization's professional development or tuition reimbursement budget can cover individual platform subscriptions for team members. Platforms like SANS OnDemand, INE, and TCM Security Academy are frequently approved for reimbursement — letting employees pursue self-directed learning without requiring a full enterprise license agreement.
</Tip>
