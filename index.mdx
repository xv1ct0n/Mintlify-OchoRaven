## How to Get Started

Not sure where to begin? Follow these four steps to go from orientation to active learning as quickly as possible.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Assess Your Skill Level">
    Be honest about where you're starting. Browse the [Learning Paths](/learning-paths) page to identify whether you belong in the Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced track — there's no shame in starting at the foundation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a Learning Path">
    Select the structured path that matches your current level and your target role. Each path spells out the topics to cover, in what order, and roughly how long each phase should take.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Resources">
    Use the Free and Paid platform directories to assemble your toolkit. Pick one primary learning platform and one certification goal to stay focused rather than spreading yourself across too many resources at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track Your Progress">
    Keep a simple log of completed modules, labs, and practice challenges. Revisiting what you've covered reinforces retention and helps you spot the gaps before they become problems on an exam or in a real engagement.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Start with free resources before committing to paid platforms. Tools like TryHackMe, Professor Messer's free CompTIA courses, and the OWASP learning materials are genuinely excellent — exhaust them first and you'll know exactly what paid depth is worth buying.
</Tip>
