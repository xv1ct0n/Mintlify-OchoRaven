Entry-level cybersecurity certifications establish foundational credibility for job seekers with zero to two years of experience, signaling to employers that you've covered the essential vocabulary, concepts, and controls that every security professional needs to know. They won't replace hands-on experience, but the right entry-level cert opens doors, satisfies HR filters, and gives you a structured curriculum to follow when self-study alone feels directionless. The certifications below represent the strongest options at this level — chosen for industry recognition, exam quality, and return on investment.

<Accordion title="CompTIA Security+">
  **What it is:** The most widely recognized entry-level security certification in the industry, covering network security, threats and vulnerabilities, cryptography, identity management, and risk management. It is compliant with DoD 8570/8140, making it a requirement for many U.S. federal and defense contractor roles.

  **Who it's for:** Career changers, IT professionals moving into security, and anyone targeting government, defense, or enterprise IT roles where Security+ is explicitly listed as a requirement or preference.

  **Exam cost:** $392 (USD)

  **Format:** Up to 90 questions (multiple choice and performance-based), 90 minutes

  **Passing score:** 750 out of 900

  **Best study resources:**
  - Professor Messer's free Security+ course (professormesser.com) — one of the best free resources available for any certification
  - Jason Dion's CompTIA Security+ course on Udemy — comprehensive and regularly updated, best purchased during a sale
  - CompTIA's official CertMaster practice exams for realistic question exposure
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Google Cybersecurity Certificate (Coursera)">
  **What it is:** A career certificate developed by Google and delivered through Coursera, covering foundational cybersecurity concepts, Linux, SQL, network security, Python scripting, and hands-on lab work using real security tools. Designed from the ground up for complete beginners.

  **Who it's for:** Anyone with zero technical background who wants a structured, affordable, and accessible entry point into cybersecurity. No prior experience or education is required.

  **Cost:** ~$49/month via Coursera (most learners complete it in 3–6 months)

  **Format:** Video lectures, quizzes, and hands-on labs across 8 courses

  **Passing requirement:** Complete all 8 courses

  **Best study resources:**
  - The Coursera program itself is the primary resource
  - Google Career Certificates community forums for peer support
  - Supplement with TryHackMe's pre-security path for additional hands-on practice
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="CompTIA A+">
  **What it is:** The foundational IT certification covering hardware, operating systems, networking, troubleshooting, and basic security concepts. While not a security-specific cert, it is a prerequisite or strong recommendation for many entry-level IT and security support roles.

  **Who it's for:** Learners with no IT background who want to build a solid technical foundation before pursuing security-specific certifications. It is particularly valuable for help desk, IT support, and desktop support roles that often serve as entry points into security careers.

  **Exam cost:** ~$246 per exam (two exams required: Core 1 and Core 2)

  **Format:** Up to 90 questions per exam (multiple choice and performance-based), 90 minutes each

  **Passing score:** 675/900 (Core 1), 700/900 (Core 2)

  **Best study resources:**
  - Professor Messer's free A+ course
  - Mike Meyers' CompTIA A+ course on Udemy
  - Jason Dion's practice exams on Udemy
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="CompTIA Network+">
  **What it is:** A vendor-neutral networking certification covering network architecture, protocols, infrastructure, security, and troubleshooting. It serves as a critical bridge between general IT skills and security-specific work, since understanding networks is foundational to nearly every security role.

  **Who it's for:** IT professionals and learners who have some foundational computer knowledge and want to build the networking literacy required for security operations, penetration testing, or cloud security work.

  **Exam cost:** $338 (USD)

  **Format:** Up to 90 questions (multiple choice and performance-based), 90 minutes

  **Passing score:** 720 out of 900

  **Best study resources:**
  - Professor Messer's free Network+ course
  - Mike Meyers' Network+ course on Udemy
  - Practice labs using Cisco Packet Tracer (free)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="eJPT (eLearnSecurity Junior Penetration Tester)">
  **What it is:** A fully hands-on, practical entry-level penetration testing certification from INE (formerly eLearnSecurity). Unlike multiple-choice exams, the eJPT requires you to complete a real penetration testing engagement in a live lab environment — making it the best first hands-on cert for aspiring pentesters.

  **Who it's for:** Learners who are interested in offensive security and want their first practical, hands-on credential. It's an excellent complement to Security+ for anyone targeting penetration testing or red team roles.

  **Exam cost:** $200 (includes two exam attempts)

  **Format:** Fully hands-on practical exam in a live lab environment (48-hour access window)

  **Passing score:** 70% (answer questions based on findings during your engagement)

  **Best study resources:**
  - INE's free Starter Pass includes the Penetration Testing Student (PTS) course that directly prepares you for the eJPT
  - TryHackMe's pre-security and Jr. Penetration Tester paths
  - TCM Security's "Practical Ethical Hacking" course as a supplement
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="ISC2 CC (Certified in Cybersecurity)">
  **What it is:** ISC2's entry-level certification covering security principles, network security, access controls, and incident response. ISC2 has offered free exam vouchers through their One Million Certified in Cybersecurity initiative, making it one of the most accessible credentials available.

  **Who it's for:** Career changers and students with no prior experience who want a globally recognized foundational credential, particularly those who may eventually pursue the CISSP later in their career and want to start in the ISC2 ecosystem early.

  **Exam cost:** Free (through the voucher program, when available); $199 standard

  **Format:** 100 multiple-choice questions, 2 hours

  **Passing score:** 700 out of 1000

  **Best study resources:**
  - ISC2's free self-paced CC training course (available on their website)
  - Thor Teaches' CC study guide on Udemy
  - ISC2 community study groups
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  CompTIA Security+ is the single most recognized entry-level certification for DoD and federal government cybersecurity roles. If you're targeting government, defense contractor, or military civilian positions, Security+ is often a hard requirement listed in the job posting. Start here if federal employment is your goal.
</Tip>
