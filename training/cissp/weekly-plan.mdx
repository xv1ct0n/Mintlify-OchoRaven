# CISSP week-by-week plan

Start with [the path overview and linked resource library](/training/cissp/index). Each week takes roughly 8–10 focused hours. Check the [official exam outline](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/cissp/cissp-certification-exam-outline) against your edition and exam appointment.

Use a simple log for each practice set: **week, domain, source, new or repeated questions, score, missed objective, exact OSG section, cause, and retest result**. Keep Quantum results separate from content-bank scores. Do not copy question text into a public repository.

| Phase | Weeks | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Content coverage | 1–12 | Read all 21 OSG chapters once; complete domain practice and spaced review. |
| Integration | 13–14 | Timed unfamiliar mixed exams, scenario reasoning, targeted remediation. |
| Readiness | 15 | Validate trends, address remaining gaps, and taper. |

## Week 01: Governance, personnel & risk

### Objective

Begin Domain 1 and establish CISSP decision making.

### Reading assignments — Official Study Guide, 10th edition

- [ ] **Chapter 1 — Security Governance Through Principles and Policies:** security concepts, governance, policy hierarchy, threat modeling, supply chain risk. Complete its review questions closed-book.
- [ ] **Chapter 2 — Personnel Security and Risk Management Concepts:** personnel policies, risk methods, social engineering, awareness and training. Complete its review questions closed-book.

### Course and reinforcement

- [ ] [Thor Pedersen Udemy Domains 1-2](https://www.udemy.com/course/cissp-domain-1-2/): Domains 1 & 2 Boot Camp — Domain 1 lessons for governance, personnel and risk; [How to Study](https://www.udemy.com/course/cissp-essentials/) for exam mindset.
- [ ] [Destination Domain 1 MindMap](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZKdGEfEyJhLd-pJhAD7dNbJyUgpqI4pu); [Pete Zerger](https://www.youtube.com/@InsideCloudAndSecurity) for due care vs. diligence, ALE/SLE/ARO, and policy vs. standard vs. procedure.

### Practice and tracking

- [ ] Chapter 1–2 review questions; 40–60 fresh Domain 1 questions across [Official Practice Tests](https://www.wiley-vch.de/en/areas-interest/computing-computer-sciences/isc2-cissp-certified-information-systems-security-professional-official-practice-tests-978-1-394-25507-8) and [LearnZapp](https://www.learnzapp.com/apps/isc2/).
- [ ] Record assessments in your assessment log, update your domain score trend, and log recurring misses in your weak-topic list. Retest misses with fresh questions after 3–7 days.

### Weekly gate

- [ ] Log baseline and explain risk terms and governance decisions; remediate any topic below 75%.
