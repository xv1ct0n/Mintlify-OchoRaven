# CISSP study path

This 15-week sequence follows the previously developed CISSP v2 plan: **12 content weeks** with no more than two new Official Study Guide (OSG) chapters each week, followed by **three weeks of mixed practice, remediation, and readiness checks**. Plan for 8–10 focused hours across four or five sessions per week. Start any week when you are ready.

## Repeatable weekly flow

1. Read each assigned OSG chapter and answer its end-of-chapter questions closed-book.
2. Complete the matching Thor domain lessons.
3. Review the corresponding Destination MindMap. Use Pete Zerger for the specific concepts listed that week.
4. Answer unfamiliar Official Practice Tests and LearnZapp questions. Start Quantum lightly in Week 9.
5. Log misses by **domain, objective, exact OSG section, cause, and next action**. Retest with fresh questions after 3–7 days.
6. Revisit completed domains after 3–5 days, again after 1–2 weeks, and during the final phase.

## Readiness signals

Aim for **75% or better on unfamiliar domain sets** before advancing, with documented remediation below that mark. In the final phase, look for **three recent unfamiliar mixed assessments around 75–80% or better**, no domain persistently below roughly 70–75%, and no major unresolved weak-topic cluster. These are study indicators, **not an ISC2 passing-score conversion**. Explain why plausible wrong answers are wrong; do not rely only on recognition or repeated question banks.

<Note>Check the official exam outline before scheduling. The plan uses relative weeks and has no calendar start or test date.</Note>
